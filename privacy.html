PRIVACY & AUDIENCE MEASUREMENT

Aggregate statistics, not personal tracking.

ProjectEngine uses Plausible Analytics solely to understand how the public website is discovered and which pages or links are useful.

What is measured

  • Page views and aggregate daily visitor counts
  • Referring websites and campaign parameters such as UTM tags
  • Browser, operating system, device category and approximate country
  • Outbound link clicks, including links to GitHub and the download page
  • 404 pages, so broken links can be repaired

What is not used

  • No cookies
  • No local storage or persistent browser identifier
  • No cross-site or cross-device tracking
  • No advertising profile or behavioural targeting
  • No sale or commercial reuse of visitor data
  • No names, email addresses, GitHub identities or free-text content

How Plausible counts visits

Plausible processes limited connection information to calculate aggregate statistics. It does not store raw IP addresses or persistent identifiers. Its daily visitor calculation uses a rotating identifier that cannot be used to follow a visitor across days, devices or websites.

The analytics service is provided by Plausible Insights OÜ and its hosted infrastructure processes and stores analytics data in the European Union.

Purpose and access

The statistics are used only to improve the ProjectEngine website, understand which communication channels are useful, detect broken navigation and evaluate aggregate interest in the open-source project.

They are not merged with advertising databases or used to identify individual visitors.

Blocking audience measurement

The site does not display a consent banner because it does not use analytics cookies or persistent tracking. Visitors who nevertheless prefer not to contribute to aggregate statistics can block requests to plausible.io using their browser or content blocker.

Contact

Questions about this website or its audience measurement can be raised through the ProjectEngine GitHub discussions.
